WHAT IS SCIENCE BOWL?

Science Bowl is a fast-paced academic competition where teams race to answer questions in biology, chemistry, physics, math, earth science, and energy. The first team to buzz in with the right answer wins the round — speed and accuracy both matter. This site gives you the tools to practice on real questions from official National Science Bowl sets before you ever step onto the competition floor.
