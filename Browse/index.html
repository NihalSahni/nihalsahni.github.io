Multiple-choice questions use the official W, X, Y, Z answer letters, and the rest are short answer, just like toss-ups and bonuses in a match.

How to use it

Browsing works best for getting a feel for a subject before you drill it. Read a few dozen questions, note the facts that keep coming up, then test yourself under time pressure in Practice or train your reaction speed on the Buzzer. Coaches can turn the same questions into a paper set with Printable Test.
