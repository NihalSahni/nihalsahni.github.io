A ONE-MINUTE TOUR OF THE SITE

Science Bowl Practice is free, needs no account, and uses real questions from official National Science Bowl sets. Here's what each tool does and a good order to try them in.

  1. 1

    PICK YOUR LEVEL

    Choose middle school or high school once, and every tool opens on your level.

    OPEN SETTINGS →
  2. 2

    STUDY THE TOPICS

    Read short guides and flip through flashcards for all six subjects: biology, chemistry, physics, earth and space, energy, and math.

    START STUDYING →
  3. 3

    TAKE A PRACTICE QUIZ

    Answer timed questions in the real W/X/Y/Z format, then review the ones you missed.

    START PRACTICING →
  4. 4

    TRAIN YOUR BUZZER

    Knowing the answer isn't enough; you have to buzz first. Practice your reaction time here.

    TRY THE BUZZER →
  5. 5

    CHECK YOUR PROGRESS

    See your accuracy by subject so you know what to study next. It saves in your browser automatically.

    VIEW PROGRESS →
  6. 6

    PRACTICE WITH YOUR TEAM

    Play live buzzer matches with friends online, or print a practice test for team meetings.

    PLAY ONLINE → PRINT A TEST →
