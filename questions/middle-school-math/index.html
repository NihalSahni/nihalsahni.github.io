MIDDLE SCHOOL MATH QUESTIONS

GRADES 6-8 · 15 REAL QUESTIONS · ANSWERS EXPLAINED

Math is one of the six Science Bowl subjects. At the middle school level it covers arithmetic, fractions, algebra, geometry and basic probability, all solved without a calculator. Below are 15 real questions from official National Science Bowl sets, each with its answer and a short explanation of why it's right. There are 311 middle school math questions in all on this site. When you're ready, practise them against the clock or review the middle school math concepts.

QUESTION 1 · SHORT ANSWER

What are the slope and y-intercept of the line containing the points (–12, –2) and (–8, 8)?

ANSWER Slope = 5/2 and y-intercept = 28

WHY Slope = (8 − (−2)) / (−8 − (−12)) = 10/4 = 5/2. Plug in (−8, 8): 8 = (5/2)(−8) + b = −20 + b, so b = 28.

QUESTION 2 · SHORT ANSWER

Convert the periodic decimal 0.8333 into a fraction

ANSWER 5/6

WHY Let x = 0.8333…. Then 100x − 10x = 83.33… − 8.33… = 75, so 90x = 75 and x = 75/90 = 5/6.

QUESTION 3 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

The lengths of two sides of a triangle are 3 and 12. Which of the following could be the length of the third side?

  • W) 4
  • X) 8
  • Y) 12
  • Z) 16

ANSWER Y) 12

WHY The triangle inequality says the third side must be between 12 − 3 = 9 and 12 + 3 = 15. Only 12 fits.

QUESTION 4 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the product of the first three even negative integers?

ANSWER -48

WHY The first three negative even integers are −2, −4 and −6. (−2)(−4)(−6) = 8 × (−6) = −48.

QUESTION 5 · SHORT ANSWER

Simplify the expression x times (2xy^2)^2

ANSWER 4x^3y^4

WHY Square the inside first: (2xy²)² = 4x²y⁴. Then multiply by x: 4x³y⁴.

QUESTION 6 · SHORT ANSWER

A squirrel runs 11 meters north, 7 meters west, and then 4 meters north. To the nearest whole meter, how far is the squirrel from its original starting point?

ANSWER 17

WHY The squirrel ends up 15 m north and 7 m west. Using the Pythagorean theorem, √(15² + 7²) = √274 ≈ 16.6, which rounds to 17 meters.

QUESTION 7 · SHORT ANSWER

The total volume of 3 identical tanks that are circular cylinders is 540π. If the height of one tank is 5, what is its radius?

ANSWER 6

WHY Each tank holds 540π / 3 = 180π. Since V = πr²h, 180π = πr²(5), so r² = 36 and r = 6.

QUESTION 8 · SHORT ANSWER

If f of x = x^3 – 3x^2 – 7x + 4, what is f of -2?

ANSWER -2

WHY f(−2) = (−8) − 3(4) − 7(−2) + 4 = −8 − 12 + 14 + 4 = −2.

QUESTION 9 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the side of a square, in centimeters to the nearest whole number, given that its diagonal is 16 centimeters?

ANSWER 11

WHY A square's diagonal is side × √2, so side = 16 / √2 = 8√2 ≈ 11.3, which rounds to 11 cm.

QUESTION 10 · SHORT ANSWER

What temperature in degrees Celsius is equivalent to 50 degrees Fahrenheit?

ANSWER 10

WHY C = (F − 32) × 5/9 = (50 − 32) × 5/9 = 18 × 5/9 = 10 °C.

QUESTION 11 · SHORT ANSWER

Name all of the following 5 numbers that are composite numbers: 3, 11, 33, 53, 63

ANSWER 33 and 63

WHY A composite number has factors other than 1 and itself. 33 = 3 × 11 and 63 = 7 × 9. The numbers 3, 11 and 53 are prime.

QUESTION 12 · SHORT ANSWER

Identify all of the following 4 sets of numbers that are Pythagorean triples: 1) 5-12-13; 2) 13-13-15; 3) 6-9-12; 4) 9-40-41

ANSWER 1 and 4

WHY Check a² + b² = c². For 5-12-13: 25 + 144 = 169 ✓. For 9-40-41: 81 + 1600 = 1681 ✓. For 6-9-12: 36 + 81 = 117, not 144. For 13-13-15: 169 + 169 = 338, not 225.

QUESTION 13 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the product of the two radical expressions 6√2 and 7√2?

ANSWER 84

WHY Multiply the outside numbers and the radicals separately: (6 × 7)(√2 × √2) = 42 × 2 = 84.

QUESTION 14 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the area of the triangle formed by the x-axis, the y-axis, and the line passing through the point (0, 8) with slope -2?

ANSWER 16

WHY The line is y = −2x + 8, which crosses the axes at (0, 8) and (4, 0). Area = ½ × 4 × 8 = 16.

QUESTION 15 · SHORT ANSWER

A bag contains 20 socks that are identical except for color: 8 are red and 12 are white. Without looking, how many socks must you take out of the bag to be certain that you will have at least one pair of white socks?

ANSWER 10

WHY In the worst case, you draw all 8 red socks first. Two more draws then have to be white, which gives a white pair. 8 + 2 = 10.

MIDDLE SCHOOL MATH STUDY TIPS

  • Practice mental math every day. There are no calculators, and many points come down to quick arithmetic.
  • Memorize the common Pythagorean triples (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25, 9-40-41) and their multiples.
  • For "how many must you take" questions, always picture the worst-case draw first.
