MIDDLE SCHOOL PHYSICS QUESTIONS

GRADES 6-8 · 15 REAL QUESTIONS · ANSWERS EXPLAINED

Physics is one of the six Science Bowl subjects. At the middle school level it covers forces and motion, energy, simple machines, waves, electricity, and atoms. Below are 15 real questions from official National Science Bowl sets, each with its answer and a short explanation of why it's right. There are 162 middle school physics questions in all on this site. When you're ready, practise them against the clock or review the middle school physics concepts.

QUESTION 1 · SHORT ANSWER

Rank the following three objects in terms of increasing momentum: 1) A one-ton car traveling at 10 meters per second; 2) A one-and-a-half-ton car traveling at 10 meters per second; 3) A one-ton car traveling at 20 meters per second

ANSWER 1, 2, 3

WHY Momentum = mass × velocity. That gives 1 × 10 = 10, 1.5 × 10 = 15, and 1 × 20 = 20 (in ton·m/s), so the order is 1, 2, 3.

QUESTION 2 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following BEST explains why a concrete floor feels colder than a wooden floor if both are at 21°C?

  • W) Wood is not as dense as concrete
  • X) Concrete conducts heat better than wood
  • Y) Wood is alive and concrete is not
  • Z) Concrete is much less elastic than wood

ANSWER X) Concrete conducts heat better than wood

WHY Both floors are the same temperature, but concrete conducts heat away from your feet faster than wood does, so it feels colder.

QUESTION 3 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

When you stretch a rubber band and hold it, what type of energy results?

  • W) Kinetic
  • X) Stationary
  • Y) Torque
  • Z) Potential

ANSWER Z) Potential

WHY A stretched rubber band stores elastic potential energy, which turns into kinetic energy when you let go.

QUESTION 4 · SHORT ANSWER

Name all of the following 3 choices that a physicist would consider to be simple machines: bicycle; lever; ramp

ANSWER Lever; ramp

WHY Levers and ramps (inclined planes) are two of the six simple machines. A bicycle is a compound machine made of several simple machines, like wheels and axles and levers.

QUESTION 5 · SHORT ANSWER

What subatomic particle is composed of two up quarks and one down quark?

ANSWER Proton

WHY A proton is two up quarks (+⅔ each) and one down quark (−⅓), for a total charge of +1. A neutron is one up and two down quarks.

QUESTION 6 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following is NOT a base unit of measure in the metric system?

  • W) Meter
  • X) Second
  • Y) Gram
  • Z) Ampere

ANSWER Y) Gram

WHY The metric (SI) base unit of mass is the kilogram, not the gram. Meters, seconds and amperes are all base units.

QUESTION 7 · SHORT ANSWER

What technology used to transmit digital information specifically takes advantage of the property of light called total internal reflection?

ANSWER Fiber optics

WHY Fiber optic cables trap light inside a glass core by total internal reflection, so pulses of light can carry data long distances.

QUESTION 8 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Many fish possess a swim bladder filled with air for what purpose?

  • W) Buoyancy
  • X) Circulation
  • Y) Digestion
  • Z) Respiration

ANSWER W) Buoyancy

WHY Fish change how much gas is in their swim bladder to adjust their buoyancy, so they can float at a depth without swimming.

QUESTION 9 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following has the most inertia?

  • W) 1 kilogram of iron
  • X) 2 kilograms of flour
  • Y) 5 newtons of iron
  • Z) 4 kilograms of iron

ANSWER Z) 4 kilograms of iron

WHY Inertia depends only on mass. The 4 kg of iron has the most. 5 newtons of iron weighs only about 0.5 kg.

QUESTION 10 · SHORT ANSWER

Name four of the six kinds of simple machines.

ANSWER Lever, wedge, pulley, wheel and axle, inclined plane, screw

WHY The six simple machines are the lever, wheel and axle, pulley, inclined plane, wedge and screw.

QUESTION 11 · SHORT ANSWER

Pascal's principle states that applied forces are evenly distributed in what state of matter?

ANSWER Liquid

WHY Pascal's principle says pressure applied to an enclosed fluid spreads equally everywhere in it. That's how hydraulic lifts and brakes work.

QUESTION 12 · SHORT ANSWER

How far will an object travel, in meters, if it is moving at 30 meters per second for 20 seconds?

ANSWER 600

WHY Distance = speed × time = 30 m/s × 20 s = 600 meters.

QUESTION 13 · SHORT ANSWER

Identify all of the following three objects that are undergoing acceleration: 1) A car braking; 2) The Moon orbiting the Earth; 3) An ice skater moving at constant velocity

ANSWER 1 and 2

WHY Acceleration is any change in speed or direction. The braking car slows down, and the Moon keeps changing direction as it orbits. The skater at constant velocity isn't accelerating.

QUESTION 14 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

What is the difference between the two stable isotopes boron-10 and boron-11?

  • W) One proton
  • X) One neutron
  • Y) Two quarks
  • Z) One electron

ANSWER X) One neutron

WHY Boron always has 5 protons. Boron-10 has 5 neutrons, and boron-11 has 6, so they differ by one neutron.

QUESTION 15 · SHORT ANSWER

Vince lifts an 80-newton box 2 meters vertically. How much work, in joules, was done on the box?

ANSWER 160

WHY Work = force × distance = 80 N × 2 m = 160 joules.

MIDDLE SCHOOL PHYSICS STUDY TIPS

  • Know the six simple machines and Newton's three laws. They show up in almost every middle school round.
  • Practice quick formulas: distance = speed × time, work = force × distance, momentum = mass × velocity.
  • Remember that inertia depends only on mass, and acceleration means any change in speed or direction.
