HIGH SCHOOL MATH QUESTIONS

GRADES 9-12 · 15 REAL QUESTIONS · ANSWERS EXPLAINED

Math is one of the six Science Bowl subjects. At the high school level it covers algebra, geometry, trigonometry, precalculus, probability and calculus, all solved without a calculator. Below are 15 real questions from official National Science Bowl sets, each with its answer and a short explanation of why it's right. There are 240 high school math questions in all on this site. When you're ready, practise them against the clock or review the high school math concepts.

QUESTION 1 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the slope of the tangent line to the function F(x) = 1000 – 200x – 5x^2 at x = 4?

ANSWER -240

WHY The tangent slope is the derivative. F′(x) = −200 − 10x, so F′(4) = −200 − 40 = −240.

QUESTION 2 · SHORT ANSWER

Find the coordinates of all points of intersection between the graphs of y = -x^2 + x and y = |3x + 3|

ANSWER None

WHY For x ≥ −1, setting −x² + x = 3x + 3 gives x² + 2x + 3 = 0, which has no real roots. For x < −1, it gives x² − 4x − 3 = 0, whose roots, 2 ± √7, aren't less than −1. So the graphs never meet.

QUESTION 3 · SHORT ANSWER

What are the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of y = -4x^2 - 16x - 9?

ANSWER (-2, 7)

WHY The vertex is at x = −b/(2a) = 16 / (−8) = −2. Then y = −4(4) − 16(−2) − 9 = −16 + 32 − 9 = 7. So the vertex is (−2, 7).

QUESTION 4 · SHORT ANSWER

Identify all of the following 4 types of quadrilaterals that can always be inscribed in a circle: 1) Parallelogram; 2) Rectangle; 3) Rhombus; 4) Kite

ANSWER 2

WHY A quadrilateral can be inscribed in a circle only if its opposite angles add up to 180°. Every rectangle's angles are 90°, so it always works. Parallelograms, rhombuses and kites only work in special cases.

QUESTION 5 · SHORT ANSWER

Solve the following equation for x: 2[x - (3x + 1)] = 4 - 2x

ANSWER -3

WHY Simplify inside the brackets: x − 3x − 1 = −2x − 1. Then 2(−2x − 1) = −4x − 2. Setting −4x − 2 = 4 − 2x gives −2x = 6, so x = −3.

QUESTION 6 · SHORT ANSWER

If (x-y)/(x+y) = 9/13, what is the value of x/y?

ANSWER 11/2

WHY Cross-multiply: 13(x − y) = 9(x + y), so 13x − 13y = 9x + 9y. That gives 4x = 22y, so x/y = 22/4 = 11/2.

QUESTION 7 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following points does the line 2x - 3y = -12 NOT pass through?

  • W) (-2, 8/3)
  • X) (1, -14/3)
  • Y) (3, 6)
  • Z) (7, 26/3)

ANSWER X) (1, -14/3)

WHY Plug each point into 2x − 3y. For (1, −14/3): 2 + 14 = 16, not −12, so the line misses it. The other three points all give −12.

QUESTION 8 · SHORT ANSWER

In degrees, what is the smallest angle x > 0 such that sin(x) + cos(2x) = 1?

ANSWER 30

WHY Use cos 2x = 1 − 2sin²x: sin x + 1 − 2sin²x = 1, so sin x(1 − 2sin x) = 0. Then sin x = 0 or sin x = 1/2. The smallest positive angle is 30°.

QUESTION 9 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following is an even function?

  • W) Sine
  • X) Cosine
  • Y) Tangent
  • Z) Cotangent

ANSWER X) Cosine

WHY An even function has f(−x) = f(x), so its graph is symmetric about the y-axis. cos(−x) = cos x, while sine, tangent and cotangent are odd.

QUESTION 10 · SHORT ANSWER

A bag contains 7 uniquely-colored marbles. Two marbles are drawn simultaneously. How many different combinations are possible?

ANSWER 21

WHY Order doesn't matter, so use combinations: C(7, 2) = (7 × 6) / 2 = 21.

QUESTION 11 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

A differentiable function f of x has domain the closed interval from 2 to 6 and range the closed interval from 4 to 8. If f prime of x is less than 0 for all x in the domain, what is f of 2?

  • W) 2
  • X) 4
  • Y) 6
  • Z) 8

ANSWER Z) 8

WHY f′(x) < 0 means f is always decreasing, so its biggest value is at the left end of the domain. f(2) is the top of the range: 8.

QUESTION 12 · SHORT ANSWER

One endpoint of a diameter of a sphere centered at (3, 4, -7) is (5, -6, 2). What are the coordinates of the other endpoint of that diameter?

ANSWER (1, 14, -16)

WHY The center is the midpoint of the diameter, so the other end = 2 × center − known end = (6 − 5, 8 + 6, −14 − 2) = (1, 14, −16).

QUESTION 13 · SHORT ANSWER

If x is inversely proportional to y^3 and if x = 3 when y = 4, what is the value of x when y = 3?

ANSWER 64/9

WHY Inverse proportion to y³ means x·y³ stays constant: 3 × 4³ = 192. So when y = 3, x = 192 / 27 = 64/9.

QUESTION 14 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following ratios represents the ratio of the diagonal of a cube to a diagonal of one of the faces of cube?

  • W) 2 to √3
  • X) √3 to √2
  • Y) √3 to 1
  • Z) √6 to √3

ANSWER X) √3 to √2

WHY For a cube with side s, the space diagonal is s√3 and a face diagonal is s√2, so the ratio is √3 to √2.

QUESTION 15 · SHORT ANSWER

A student has a paper cup in the shape of a right circular cone, with a height of 5 inches and an opening diameter of 3 inches. How many of these paper cups of water will it take to fill a cylindrical container of diameter 3 inches and height 10 inches?

ANSWER 6

WHY A cone's volume is ⅓πr²h = ⅓πr²(5). The cylinder's is πr²(10), with the same radius. Dividing: 10 / (5/3) = 6 cups.

HIGH SCHOOL MATH STUDY TIPS

  • Speed matters more than difficulty. Practice common moves until they're instant: vertex formula, combinations, derivatives of polynomials.
  • Plugging in often beats solving. On multiple-choice questions, testing each option is usually the fastest route.
  • Memorize key facts like cube and face diagonals, trig values at 30°, 45° and 60°, and the cone-to-cylinder volume ratio of 1 to 3.
