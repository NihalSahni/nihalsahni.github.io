MIDDLE SCHOOL CHEMISTRY QUESTIONS

GRADES 6-8 · 15 REAL QUESTIONS · ANSWERS EXPLAINED

Chemistry is one of the six Science Bowl subjects. At the middle school level it covers atoms, the periodic table, chemical and physical changes, acids and bases, and lab basics. Below are 15 real questions from official National Science Bowl sets, each with its answer and a short explanation of why it's right. There are 84 middle school chemistry questions in all on this site. When you're ready, practise them against the clock or review the middle school chemistry concepts.

QUESTION 1 · SHORT ANSWER

Dry ice is the solid form of what molecule?

ANSWER Carbon dioxide

WHY Dry ice is frozen carbon dioxide (CO₂). At normal pressure it sublimates, going straight from solid to gas at −78.5 °C.

QUESTION 2 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following is never considered evidence of a chemical change?

  • W) Phase change
  • X) Color change
  • Y) Formation of a precipitate
  • Z) Formation of a gas

ANSWER W) Phase change

WHY A phase change, like ice melting, is a physical change: the molecules stay the same. Color changes, precipitates and gas bubbles can all signal a new substance.

QUESTION 3 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following represents a chemical property of sodium?

  • W) It is less dense than water
  • X) It has a melting point of 98.7 degrees Celsius
  • Y) It reacts with water to release hydrogen gas
  • Z) When cut with a knife, it has a shiny surface

ANSWER Y) It reacts with water to release hydrogen gas

WHY A chemical property describes how a substance reacts to become something new. Sodium reacting with water to make hydrogen gas is one. Density, melting point and shininess are physical properties.

QUESTION 4 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following is NOT a strong acid?

  • W) Hydrochloric Acid
  • X) Phosphoric Acid
  • Y) Sulfuric Acid
  • Z) Perchloric Acid

ANSWER X) Phosphoric Acid

WHY Phosphoric acid (H₃PO₄) only partly ionizes in water, so it's a weak acid. Hydrochloric, sulfuric and perchloric acids ionize completely.

QUESTION 5 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the name for the voltaic cells in which the reactants are continuously supplied to the cell and the products are continuously removed?

ANSWER Fuel cell

WHY A fuel cell is a voltaic cell that keeps running as long as fuel (like hydrogen) and oxygen are supplied, unlike a battery, which runs out.

QUESTION 6 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

A Lewis base is defined as any species that does which of the following in a chemical reaction?

  • W) Donates a proton
  • X) Accepts a proton
  • Y) Donates a pair of electrons
  • Z) Accepts a pair of electrons

ANSWER Y) Donates a pair of electrons

WHY A Lewis base donates an electron pair, and a Lewis acid accepts one. Ammonia (NH₃) is a classic Lewis base because of its lone pair.

QUESTION 7 · SHORT ANSWER

Which one of the following 5 atoms can violate the octet rule with more than 8 electrons: O; F; Cl; N; C

ANSWER Cl

WHY Elements in period 3 and below, like chlorine, can hold more than 8 valence electrons, as in ClF₃. Period-2 elements like C, N, O and F can't.

QUESTION 8 · SHORT ANSWER

The molecular weight of sodium is 22.99 grams per mole, and the molecular weight of chlorine is 35.45 grams per mole. What is the molecular weight in grams per mole of sodium chloride?

ANSWER 58.44

WHY Add the two atomic masses: 22.99 + 35.45 = 58.44 g/mol.

QUESTION 9 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the oxidation state of hydrogen atoms in ammonia?

ANSWER +1

WHY Nitrogen is more electronegative than hydrogen, so it's assigned −3, and each of the three hydrogens is +1. The total is −3 + 3(+1) = 0.

QUESTION 10 · SHORT ANSWER

What hard silvery-grey metal is the 9th most common element in the Earth's crust, has an atomic number of 22, and takes its name from the Titans?

ANSWER Titanium

WHY Titanium (Ti, atomic number 22) is named after the Titans of Greek mythology. It's strong, light and resists corrosion, which makes it useful in aircraft and medical implants.

QUESTION 11 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the most abundant element in the universe?

ANSWER Hydrogen

WHY Hydrogen makes up about 74% of the normal matter in the universe by mass, and most of the atoms.

QUESTION 12 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following elements would be expected to possess very different chemical properties from the others based on its placement in the periodic table?

  • W) Chlorine
  • X) Argon
  • Y) Iodine
  • Z) Fluorine

ANSWER X) Argon

WHY Argon is a noble gas with a full outer shell, so it barely reacts. Chlorine, iodine and fluorine are all halogens, among the most reactive elements.

QUESTION 13 · SHORT ANSWER

From what biological polymer is paper primarily composed?

ANSWER Cellulose

WHY Paper is mostly cellulose, the plant fiber polymer made of linked glucose units, usually from wood pulp.

QUESTION 14 · SHORT ANSWER

What element makes up about 24% of the Sun's mass?

ANSWER Helium

WHY The Sun is about 73% hydrogen and about 25% helium by mass. Fusion in its core keeps turning hydrogen into helium.

QUESTION 15 · SHORT ANSWER

How many micrograms are in a single milligram?

ANSWER 1000

WHY "Milli" means one thousandth and "micro" means one millionth, so 1 milligram = 1,000 micrograms.

MIDDLE SCHOOL CHEMISTRY STUDY TIPS

  • Learn the periodic table groups: alkali metals, halogens and noble gases, and why elements in the same group act alike.
  • Practice telling physical changes from chemical changes, and physical properties from chemical properties. These questions are easy points.
  • Know metric prefixes (kilo, centi, milli, micro) so unit conversions take seconds.
