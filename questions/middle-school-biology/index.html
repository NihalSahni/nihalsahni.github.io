MIDDLE SCHOOL BIOLOGY QUESTIONS

GRADES 6-8 · 15 REAL QUESTIONS · ANSWERS EXPLAINED

Biology is one of the six Science Bowl subjects. At the middle school level it covers cells, the human body, plants, genetics, ecology and classification. Below are 15 real questions from official National Science Bowl sets, each with its answer and a short explanation of why it's right. There are 226 middle school biology questions in all on this site. When you're ready, practise them against the clock or review the middle school biology concepts.

QUESTION 1 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

A diver finds an underwater cave filled with bacteria that seem to feed on volcanic sulfur vents. What type of organisms are these?

  • W) Heterotroph
  • X) Hemotroph
  • Y) Chemotroph
  • Z) Phototroph

ANSWER Y) Chemotroph

WHY Chemotrophs get their energy from chemicals instead of sunlight. Bacteria at deep-sea vents and in sulfur caves use chemosynthesis, breaking down compounds like hydrogen sulfide to make food.

QUESTION 2 · SHORT ANSWER

What specific structure is most directly responsible for the motility of bacteria?

ANSWER Flagella

WHY Bacteria swim by spinning long, whip-like flagella that work like tiny propellers.

QUESTION 3 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

What do we call the evaporation of water from plants that occurs chiefly while their stomata are open?

  • W) Transpiration
  • X) Chemotaxis
  • Y) Chemosynthesis
  • Z) Chlorophyll

ANSWER W) Transpiration

WHY Transpiration is water evaporating from leaves, mostly through open stomata. It pulls water up from the roots through the xylem.

QUESTION 4 · SHORT ANSWER

Several species of birds feed on ectoparasites that they clean off of large mammals. This behavior is what kind of interaction?

ANSWER Mutualism

WHY Both species benefit: the birds get food, and the mammals get rid of ticks and other parasites. Oxpeckers on rhinos and zebras are a classic example.

QUESTION 5 · SHORT ANSWER

On a sunny day, if the stomata on a plant leaf are forced to close, what two molecules would be prevented from leaving the leaf?

ANSWER Oxygen and water

WHY Stomata let gases in and out of the leaf. Closing them traps the oxygen made by photosynthesis and the water vapor that would leave by transpiration. It also keeps carbon dioxide from getting in.

QUESTION 6 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the term for the middle portion of the arthropod body to which the wings are usually attached?

ANSWER Thorax

WHY An insect's body has three parts: head, thorax and abdomen. The legs and wings attach to the thorax.

QUESTION 7 · SHORT ANSWER

What vitamin is the most directly associated with the disease scurvy?

ANSWER Vitamin C

WHY Scurvy comes from a lack of vitamin C (ascorbic acid), which the body needs to make collagen. Sailors used to prevent it by eating citrus fruit.

QUESTION 8 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

When crossing two heterozygous plants, what percentage of the offspring do you expect to be homozygous?

  • W) 0
  • X) 25
  • Y) 50
  • Z) 75

ANSWER Y) 50

WHY Aa × Aa gives AA, Aa, Aa, aa. AA and aa are homozygous: 2 out of 4, or 50%.

QUESTION 9 · SHORT ANSWER

The organ of Corti is directly involved in what human sense?

ANSWER Hearing

WHY The organ of Corti sits in the cochlea of the inner ear. Its hair cells turn sound vibrations into nerve signals.

QUESTION 10 · SHORT ANSWER

What is the common specific name for the bond that joins two amino acids to form a growing protein?

ANSWER Peptide bond

WHY A peptide bond forms between the carboxyl group of one amino acid and the amino group of the next, releasing a water molecule.

QUESTION 11 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following organisms evolved most recently on Earth?

  • W) Reptiles
  • X) Insects
  • Y) Mammals
  • Z) Fish

ANSWER Y) Mammals

WHY Fish appeared first (over 500 million years ago), then insects, then reptiles. Mammals came last, about 200 million years ago.

QUESTION 12 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Most extremophiles belong to which group of organisms?

  • W) Protists
  • X) Fungi
  • Y) Eukaryotes
  • Z) Archaea

ANSWER Z) Archaea

WHY Archaea are single-celled microbes known for living in extreme places like hot springs, salt lakes and deep-sea vents. Many extremophiles are archaea.

QUESTION 13 · SHORT ANSWER

In a marine environment, what name is given to the collective group of organisms that serve as primary producers?

ANSWER Phytoplankton

WHY Phytoplankton are tiny floating algae and cyanobacteria. They photosynthesize, form the base of ocean food webs, and produce a large share of Earth's oxygen.

QUESTION 14 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

What part of the brain plays a major role in endocrine regulation, and is primarily responsible for maintaining homeostasis?

  • W) Pons
  • X) Cerebellum
  • Y) Thalamus
  • Z) Hypothalamus

ANSWER Z) Hypothalamus

WHY The hypothalamus links the nervous system to the endocrine system by controlling the pituitary gland. It regulates body temperature, hunger and thirst.

QUESTION 15 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following portions of the seed serves as the plant embryo's food supply until germination?

  • W) Pollen
  • X) Embryo
  • Y) Endosperm
  • Z) Gametophyte

ANSWER Y) Endosperm

WHY The endosperm is stored food, mostly starch, that feeds the young plant until it can photosynthesize. It's the main part of grains like wheat and corn.

MIDDLE SCHOOL BIOLOGY STUDY TIPS

  • Know the parts of a cell and what each one does. Organelle questions show up in almost every round.
  • Learn the main human body systems and their key organs, like the hypothalamus, the cochlea and the placenta.
  • For ecology, practice naming relationships: mutualism, parasitism, commensalism, and predator and prey.
