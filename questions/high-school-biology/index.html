HIGH SCHOOL BIOLOGY QUESTIONS

GRADES 9-12 · 15 REAL QUESTIONS · ANSWERS EXPLAINED

Biology is one of the six Science Bowl subjects. At the high school level it covers cell biology, genetics, human physiology, ecology, evolution and classification. Below are 15 real questions from official National Science Bowl sets, each with its answer and a short explanation of why it's right. There are 260 high school biology questions in all on this site. When you're ready, practise them against the clock or review the high school biology concepts.

QUESTION 1 · SHORT ANSWER

Atherosclerotic plaques are composed of what lipid stored in LDL and HDL particles?

ANSWER Cholesterol

WHY LDL and HDL are lipoproteins that carry cholesterol through the blood. When too much LDL cholesterol builds up in artery walls, it forms the plaques of atherosclerosis.

QUESTION 2 · SHORT ANSWER

What chromosome are Barr bodies derived from?

ANSWER X

WHY A Barr body is an inactivated, tightly coiled X chromosome. Female mammals have two X chromosomes, and one is switched off in each cell so both sexes get the same dose of X-linked genes.

QUESTION 3 · SHORT ANSWER

If a plant that is heterozygous for each of three independently-assorting traits is allowed to self-fertilize, what proportion of the offspring would be heterozygous for all three traits?

ANSWER 1/8

WHY For each trait, Aa × Aa gives Aa half the time. Because the three traits assort independently, multiply: 1/2 × 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/8.

QUESTION 4 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

Which of the following groups of organisms would contain the most biomass productivity in a trophic pyramid?

  • W) Primary Producers
  • X) Primary Consumers
  • Y) Detritivores
  • Z) Tertiary Consumers

ANSWER W) Primary Producers

WHY Producers sit at the base of the pyramid. Only about 10% of the energy at each level passes up to the next, so producers always hold the most biomass productivity.

QUESTION 5 · SHORT ANSWER

What generic group of transport proteins facilitates the diffusion of water across membranes?

ANSWER Aquaporin

WHY Aquaporins are channel proteins that let water move through the membrane far faster than it could cross the lipid bilayer alone. They are especially important in kidney cells.

QUESTION 6 · SHORT ANSWER

The nucleolus in eukaryotic cells is the site of the synthesis of the components of what organelle?

ANSWER Ribosome

WHY The nucleolus makes ribosomal RNA (rRNA) and assembles it with proteins into ribosome subunits, which then leave the nucleus to make proteins in the cytoplasm.

QUESTION 7 · SHORT ANSWER

In what human organ can one find cells possessing intercalated disks?

ANSWER Heart

WHY Intercalated disks connect cardiac muscle cells. They contain gap junctions that let electrical signals pass quickly from cell to cell so the heart contracts as one unit.

QUESTION 8 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

All of the following changes would result in an increase in blood pressure, except:

  • W) Increased cardiac output
  • X) Increased heart rate
  • Y) Increased diameter of arterioles
  • Z) Increased stroke volume

ANSWER Y) Increased diameter of arterioles

WHY Widening the arterioles (vasodilation) lowers resistance to blood flow, which lowers blood pressure. Pumping more blood, faster or with bigger strokes, raises it.

QUESTION 9 · SHORT ANSWER

A man is born with webbed fingers and toes. His wife and their daughter do not have webbed fingers and toes. If webbed digits are a dominant trait, what fraction of this couple's children would be expected to have webbed digits?

ANSWER 1/2

WHY Their daughter doesn't have webbed digits, so she got a recessive allele from her father. That makes him heterozygous (Ww). Ww × ww gives Ww half the time, so 1/2 of their children are expected to have webbed digits.

QUESTION 10 · SHORT ANSWER

In what chamber of the human heart is the sinoatrial node located?

ANSWER Right atrium

WHY The sinoatrial (SA) node, the heart's natural pacemaker, sits in the wall of the right atrium. It starts each heartbeat.

QUESTION 11 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

The oxygen released into the air as a product of photosynthesis comes from what?

  • W) Carbon dioxide
  • X) Water
  • Y) Glucose
  • Z) Rubisco

ANSWER X) Water

WHY In the light reactions, water is split to replace electrons lost by chlorophyll, and that releases O₂. Experiments with oxygen-18 showed the oxygen comes from water, not CO₂.

QUESTION 12 · SHORT ANSWER

Arrange the following four groupings of organisms from narrowest to broadest level of categorization: 1) genus, 2) phylum, 3) domain, 4) class

ANSWER 1, 4, 2, 3

WHY From broadest to narrowest, taxonomy goes domain, kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, species. Reversed and narrowed to these four: genus, class, phylum, domain.

QUESTION 13 · SHORT ANSWER

A simplified food chain for the Aleutian Islands consists of killer whales, sea otters, sea urchins, and kelp. Reducing the number of sea otters is likely to have what impact on the population of kelp?

ANSWER Reduction

WHY This is a trophic cascade. Sea otters eat sea urchins, and urchins eat kelp. Fewer otters means more urchins, and more urchins means less kelp.

QUESTION 14 · MULTIPLE CHOICE

The thumb's opposability can be attributed to what type of synovial joint?

  • W) Pivot joint
  • X) Saddle joint
  • Y) Hinge joint
  • Z) Condyloid joint

ANSWER X) Saddle joint

WHY The joint at the base of the thumb (the carpometacarpal joint) is a saddle joint. Its two saddle-shaped surfaces let the thumb move in two directions and swing across the palm.

QUESTION 15 · SHORT ANSWER

Identify all of the following three structures that are homologous to the human arm: 1) Bat wing; 2) Cat hind leg; 3) Porpoise front flipper

ANSWER 1, 3

WHY Homologous structures share the same bone layout because they come from a common ancestor. A bat wing and a porpoise flipper are front limbs built on the same plan as the human arm. A cat's hind leg matches the human leg instead.

HIGH SCHOOL BIOLOGY STUDY TIPS

  • Know the human body well. Organs, hormones and exactly where things happen (like the SA node in the right atrium) come up again and again.
  • For genetics problems, write out the cross. Most probability questions come down to multiplying simple fractions like 1/2 and 1/4.
  • Learn word roots. A word like "capnophile" makes sense once you know capno- means smoke and -phile means loving.
